BETTER TOGETHER

Read it, test it, close the loop.

The book and the question bank follow the same subjects and the same teaching pearls, so each one sends you back to the other.

  1. Read the chapter

    Start a subject in Boardside Notes: presentation, diagnosis and management for each disease, at a glance.

  2. Do the questions

    Then do that subject in the question bank in learning mode, untimed, while the chapter is fresh.

  3. Go back to the book for every miss

    Find the disease in the book, reread the pearl the question turned on, and note the trap in the margin.

  4. Finish in exam mode

    Near your exam, switch to timed, mixed-subject blocks, and use the book's rapid-review list the week before.

HOW IT WORKS

Learn from every answer choice, not just the right one.

1

Commit to an answer

Every question opens on a full board-style vignette. Read it like the exam, and pick one.

2

The explanation walks through the answer the way an attending teaches it on rounds, with a teaching pearl to remember.

3

Learn why not

Each wrong option gets its own sentence: the clue it ignores, or when it would be right.
