How to play

  1. Every day at midnight Pacific there's a new board. Everyone gets the same one.
  2. Find the theme words hidden in the letters. Drag across letters, or tap them one by one and tap the last letter again to submit. Words can bend in any direction, including diagonally.
  3. One word is the Bundle. It names the theme and runs from one side of the board to the other.
  4. Theme words fill the board exactly. Every letter is used once, so when you're done, nothing is left over.
  5. Stuck? Every 3 other real words you find (4+ letters) earn a hint that outlines one theme word's letters.
  6. Solve without a hint to post your time to the daily no-hint leaderboard. The clock starts at your first letter.
