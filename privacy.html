Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Who we are

Boardside Reviews (“Boardside,” “we,” “us”) runs boardsidereviews.com, an internal medicine question bank, book and set of learning games. Boardside is operated by Khaled M. Abdullah, MD.

This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using boardsidereviews.com, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run Boardside.

  • Account details: first and last name, email address, and password. Passwords are stored in scrambled (hashed) form, so we cannot see them.
  • Your choices at sign-up: whether you join our mailing list, and your answer to “How did you hear about us?”
  • Purchases: the plan or book you bought, the price, and the date. Card details are entered directly with our payment processor, Stripe. We never receive or store your full card number.
  • Book orders: you complete your book order on Lulu’s website, where Lulu collects your shipping address. We receive basic order details from Lulu so we can help with any delivery problem.
  • Question bank activity: your answers, scores, flagged questions, confidence ratings, flashcard progress, and settings.
  • Games: if you choose to post a score, your score with your first name and last initial.
  • Group sessions: only combined averages for the group. We do not save individual answers from group sessions.
  • Messages: anything you send to our support, hello, or partners email addresses.
  • Site usage: pages visited, device and browser type, and approximate location, collected through Google Analytics.
  • Refund Guarantee claims: the score report you send us (see below).

How we use your information

We use your information only to run Boardside and to email you if you ask us to.

  • Create and manage your account, and give you access to what you bought.
  • Run the question bank, flashcards, games, leaderboards, and group sessions.
  • Process payments, and send you to Lulu to order the book.
  • Send service emails, such as verification codes, password resets, receipts, a welcome email, and important account notices. These go to every account holder, whether or not you join the mailing list.
  • Send mailing list emails, only if you opt in.
  • Answer your messages and review Refund Guarantee claims.
  • Understand how the site is used so we can fix problems and improve it.
  • Prevent fraud and account sharing, and meet our legal obligations.

Our mailing list

You join our mailing list only if you tick the box at sign-up. It is never ticked for you.

Mailing list emails share updates, new features, study tips, and offers from Boardside. Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom, or you can email support@boardsidereviews.com. Unsubscribing does not affect your account or your access. You will still receive service emails about your account and purchases.

We do not sell your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not give it to other organizations for their own marketing.

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except the service providers below, which run parts of Boardside for us. Each receives only what it needs to do that job, and handles it under its own privacy policy:

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect the rights or safety of Boardside or others, or if Boardside is sold or transferred. Any new owner would be bound by this policy.

What other users can see: your first name, last initial, and score on game leaderboards, only if you choose to post a score. Question bank scores are never shown publicly. If your residency program bought access for your class, your chief residents see only anonymous class averages, never your individual results.

Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar browser storage to keep you signed in and remember your settings.

Google Analytics uses cookies to count visits. We do not use advertising trackers or pixels. You can block cookies in your browser, or opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s opt-out add-on.

Our homepage shows embedded Instagram posts. When they load, Instagram (Meta) may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy.

Do Not Track: our site does not currently respond to browser Do Not Track signals.

Refund Guarantee score reports

We use the score report you send for a Refund Guarantee claim only to confirm that you qualify. We check the name, the exam result, and the score release date.

You may black out anything we don’t need, such as your ABIM ID number. We do not share your score report with anyone, and we delete it once your claim is decided.

How long we keep your information

We keep your account information while your account is open.

You can delete your account at any time from your account menu (Profile info → Delete account). We then delete your personal information and question bank progress within 30 days. Deleting your account is permanent: your progress is lost, your access ends, and creating a new account with the same email does not restore it. We keep purchase records, such as receipts and order details, indefinitely so our tax and accounting records stay complete.

Google Analytics keeps event data for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to help you.

Your choices and rights

You are in control of your information.

  • Update your name or email in your profile.
  • Unsubscribe from the mailing list at any time.
  • Access, correct, or delete: email support@boardsidereviews.com to request a copy of your information, a correction, or deletion. We reply within 45 days and may need to confirm your identity first.
  • California residents: we do not share personal information with other businesses for their direct marketing, so we have nothing to report under California’s “Shine the Light” law. You may still ask us about it.

We will never treat you differently for using any of these rights.

Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and access limited to what is needed to run Boardside. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you as the law requires. Please keep your password private.

Children

Boardside is made for medical trainees and physicians, and you must be at least 18 to create an account. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy. When we do, we will post the new version here with a new effective date. If the changes are significant, we will also email account holders.
