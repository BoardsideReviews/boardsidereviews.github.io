How to play

  1. Every day at midnight Pacific there's a new landmark trial from a top journal. Everyone gets the same one.
  2. Read the design: who was enrolled, what was tested, against what, and the primary outcome.
  3. Call the result in one guess:
    POSITIVE: the intervention met its primary endpoint (for noninferiority trials, it was shown noninferior).
    NEUTRAL: no significant difference on the primary endpoint.
    NEGATIVE: the intervention did worse, or the trial was stopped early for harm.
  4. Then read the result, the bottom line, and the original article.
  5. Get it right on consecutive days to build your streak.
