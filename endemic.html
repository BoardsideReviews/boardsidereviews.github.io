How to play

  1. Every day at midnight Pacific there's a new pathogen or disease, the same one for everyone.
  2. Read the clue, then tap the map to drop one pin where it's endemic. Zoom in for precision. You can move the pin until you lock it in.
  3. Land inside the endemic area for a perfect 5,000. Outside, you lose points the farther you are from the nearest endemic zone.
  4. Then see the real map, the teaching pearl, and today's leaderboard.
