# Differentials and classic associations
Differential|Low potassium|HYPOKALEMIA|DIURETICS VOMITING DIARRHEA INSULIN ALBUTEROL ALDOSTERONISM MAGNESIUM BARTTER|Hypokalemia with hypertension suggests mineralocorticoid excess; check renin and aldosterone.
Differential|High potassium|HYPERKALEMIA|HEMOLYSIS ACIDOSIS SPIRONOLACTONE TRIMETHOPRIM HEPARIN RHABDOMYOLYSIS SUCCINYLCHOLINE|Trimethoprim blocks ENaC like amiloride and can raise potassium, especially in kidney disease.
Differential|Low sodium workup|HYPOTONIC|OSMOLALITY VOLUME URINESODIUM SIADH THIAZIDE PSEUDO HYPERGLYCEMIA HYPOTHYROID|Correct sodium for hyperglycemia: add about 1.6–2.4 mEq/L for every 100 mg/dL of glucose above normal.
Differential|Pancytopenia|PANCYTOPENIA|APLASTIC LEUKEMIA MYELODYSPLASIA BTWELVE HYPERSPLENISM LUPUS HIV DRUGS|Pancytopenia with a big spleen points to hypersplenism, myelofibrosis, or lymphoma; a small spleen points to marrow failure.
Differential|Big spleen|SPLENOMEGALY|PORTALHYPERTENSION MALARIA LEISHMANIA MYELOFIBROSIS LEUKEMIA LYMPHOMA MONO GAUCHER|Massive splenomegaly: CML, myelofibrosis, malaria, kala-azar, Gaucher disease, and hairy cell leukemia.
Differential|Fever without a source|FEVERUNKNOWN|ENDOCARDITIS TUBERCULOSIS LYMPHOMA ABSCESS GIANTCELL STILLS DRUGFEVER FACTITIOUS|In older adults with fever of unknown origin, consider giant cell arteritis even without headache.
Differential|Hilar nodes|HILARNODES|SARCOIDOSIS TUBERCULOSIS LYMPHOMA HISTOPLASMOSIS SILICOSIS BERYLLIOSIS METASTASIS|Eggshell calcification of hilar nodes suggests silicosis or sarcoidosis.
Differential|Cavities on CT|CAVITARY|TUBERCULOSIS SQUAMOUS ABSCESS GRANULOMATOSIS SEPTICEMBOLI KLEBSIELLA ASPERGILLOMA NOCARDIA|Multiple peripheral cavitary nodules in an injection drug user suggest septic emboli from tricuspid endocarditis.
Differential|Ground-glass|GROUNDGLASS|PNEUMOCYSTIS EDEMA HEMORRHAGE HYPERSENSITIVITY COVID ORGANIZING VAPING|Diffuse ground-glass opacities with a low CD4 count suggest Pneumocystis pneumonia.
Differential|Exudative effusion|EXUDATES|PARAPNEUMONIC MALIGNANCY TUBERCULOSIS EMBOLISM PANCREATITIS LUPUS RHEUMATOID ESOPHAGEAL|A very low pleural glucose suggests rheumatoid effusion, empyema, TB, malignancy, or esophageal rupture.
Differential|Wheezy and eosinophils|EOSINOPHILICLUNG|ASPERGILLOSIS CHURGSTRAUSS LOEFFLER STRONGYLOIDES DRUGREACTION CHRONIC ACUTE|Acute eosinophilic pneumonia has been linked to new-onset smoking and vaping.
Differential|Clubbing and cyanosis|CYANOSIS|SHUNT METHEMOGLOBIN HYPOXEMIA POLYCYTHEMIA EISENMENGER PERIPHERAL CENTRAL|Cyanosis needs about 5 g/dL of deoxygenated hemoglobin, so it is harder to see in anemia.
Differential|High ammonia|HYPERAMMONEMIA|CIRRHOSIS VALPROATE UREACYCLE SHUNT INFECTION GIBLEED|Valproate can raise ammonia and cause encephalopathy even with normal liver tests.
Differential|Elevated lactate|LACTICACIDOSIS|SEPSIS SHOCK SEIZURE METFORMIN LINEZOLID CYANIDE THIAMINE MALIGNANCY|Type B lactic acidosis (without shock) comes from drugs like metformin, thiamine deficiency, or malignancy.
Differential|Raised CK|CREATINEKINASE|RHABDOMYOLYSIS STATINS MYOSITIS HYPOTHYROIDISM EXERCISE SEIZURE INJECTION TRAUMA|Hypothyroidism is a common, easily missed cause of a mildly elevated CK.
Differential|Prolonged PT|COAGULOPATHY|WARFARIN LIVER VITAMINK MALNUTRITION ANTIBIOTICS DIC FACTOR SEVEN|An isolated prolonged PT points to factor VII deficiency, early vitamin K deficiency, or warfarin.
Differential|Hyperpigmentation|PIGMENTATION|ADDISON HEMOCHROMATOSIS AMIODARONE MINOCYCLINE PORPHYRIA ACANTHOSIS MELASMA|Amiodarone causes a blue-gray skin discoloration in sun-exposed areas.
Differential|Pink urine|DISCOLOREDURINE|HEMOGLOBIN MYOGLOBIN RIFAMPIN BEETS PORPHYRIA PHENAZOPYRIDINE PROPOFOL|Propofol can turn urine green; rifampin turns it orange-red.
Differential|Hypercoagulable cancer|TROUSSEAU|PANCREATIC MIGRATORY THROMBOPHLEBITIS MUCIN ADENOCARCINOMA HEPARIN|Trousseau syndrome (migratory thrombophlebitis) from mucin-producing adenocarcinomas responds better to heparin than warfarin.
Differential|Pulmonary hypertension in liver|PORTOPULMONARY|CIRRHOSIS PORTAL VASODILATOR TRANSPLANT HEPATOPULMONARY ORTHODEOXIA SHUNT|Hepatopulmonary syndrome causes hypoxemia from intrapulmonary shunting; portopulmonary hypertension causes high PA pressures.
Differential|Hypoxia when upright|ORTHODEOXIA|PLATYPNEA HEPATOPULMONARY FORAMENOVALE BUBBLESTUDY SHUNT BASES|Orthodeoxia (oxygen falls when upright) suggests hepatopulmonary syndrome or an intracardiac shunt.
Differential|Hypertension in the young|SECONDARYHTN|ALDOSTERONISM RENOVASCULAR COARCTATION PHEOCHROMOCYTOMA CUSHING SLEEPAPNEA THYROID|Primary aldosteronism is the most common cause of secondary hypertension.
Differential|Polyuria|POLYURIA|DIABETES INSIPIDUS HYPERGLYCEMIA DIURETICS HYPERCALCEMIA POLYDIPSIA POSTOBSTRUCTIVE LITHIUM|A water deprivation test separates primary polydipsia from central and nephrogenic diabetes insipidus.
Differential|Pruritus without rash|ITCHING|CHOLESTASIS UREMIA POLYCYTHEMIA LYMPHOMA IRON THYROID OPIOIDS|Itching after a hot shower (aquagenic pruritus) is a classic clue to polycythemia vera.
Differential|Hiccups that won't quit|HICCUPS|PHRENIC DIAPHRAGM UREMIA BRAINSTEM BACLOFEN CHLORPROMAZINE GASTRIC PNEUMONIA|Persistent hiccups beyond 48 hours warrant a search for CNS, thoracic, or metabolic causes.
Differential|Sudden vision loss|VISIONLOSS|ARTERYOCCLUSION VEIN DETACHMENT GIANTCELL STROKE VITREOUS GLAUCOMA|Sudden vision loss in an older adult with headache and jaw claudication is giant cell arteritis until proven otherwise.
Differential|Abdominal pain mimics|MEDICALBELLY|DKA PORPHYRIA LEAD SICKLE INFERIORMI PNEUMONIA ADRENAL ANGIOEDEMA|DKA, inferior MI, lower-lobe pneumonia, and acute porphyria can all mimic a surgical abdomen.
Differential|Purple urine and pain|PORPHYRIA|AMINOLEVULINIC PORPHOBILINOGEN HEMIN NEUROPATHY ABDOMINALPAIN HYPONATREMIA SEIZURES|Acute intermittent porphyria attacks are triggered by drugs, fasting, and hormones; treat with IV hemin and glucose.
Differential|Weakness and potassium|PERIODICPARALYSIS|HYPOKALEMIC THYROTOXIC CARBOHYDRATE EXERCISE PROPRANOLOL POTASSIUM|Thyrotoxic periodic paralysis: sudden weakness with low potassium in Asian men with hyperthyroidism.
Differential|Gynecomastia|GYNECOMASTIA|SPIRONOLACTONE CIRRHOSIS KETOCONAZOLE DIGOXIN TESTICULAR PUBERTY KLINEFELTER|Spironolactone causes gynecomastia; switching to eplerenone avoids it.
Differential|Dementia mimics|REVERSIBLEDEMENTIA|BTWELVE THYROID SYPHILIS DEPRESSION HYDROCEPHALUS SUBDURAL|Check B12 and TSH in every new dementia workup; consider syphilis testing in at-risk patients.
Differential|Hypotension in ICU|SHOCKTYPES|SEPTIC CARDIOGENIC HYPOVOLEMIC OBSTRUCTIVE ANAPHYLACTIC ADRENAL NEUROGENIC|Distributive shock is warm with a high cardiac output; cardiogenic and hypovolemic shock are cold.
Differential|Low blood pressure, slow heart|BRADYHYPOTENSION|BETABLOCKER CALCIUMBLOCKER DIGOXIN HYPERKALEMIA HYPOTHERMIA NEUROGENIC|Bradycardia with hypotension: think drugs (beta-blockers, calcium channel blockers, digoxin), hyperkalemia, or spinal cord injury.
Differential|Hypoglycemia without diabetes|LOWSUGAR|INSULINOMA ADRENAL LIVERFAILURE SEPSIS ALCOHOL SULFONYLUREA BARIATRIC|Postprandial hypoglycemia after gastric bypass comes from late insulin surges (dumping).
Differential|Diarrhea for weeks|CHRONICDIARRHEA|CELIAC MICROSCOPIC COLITIS GIARDIA THYROID LACTOSE BILEACID CARCINOID|Microscopic colitis: chronic watery diarrhea in older women with a normal-looking colonoscopy; biopsy makes the diagnosis.
Differential|Constipation causes|CONSTIPATION|OPIOIDS HYPOTHYROIDISM HYPERCALCEMIA PARKINSON DIABETES IRON CALCIUMBLOCKER|Opioid-induced constipation can be treated with peripherally acting mu antagonists such as methylnaltrexone.
Differential|Jaundice types|HYPERBILIRUBINEMIA|GILBERT HEMOLYSIS CRIGLER OBSTRUCTION HEPATITIS DUBINJOHNSON ROTOR|Gilbert syndrome: mild unconjugated hyperbilirubinemia that rises with fasting or illness; benign.
Differential|Elevated alkaline phosphatase|ALKPHOS|BONE PAGET CHOLESTASIS PREGNANCY METASTASES OSTEOMALACIA GROWTH|An elevated alkaline phosphatase with a normal GGT points to bone (Paget disease, metastases, osteomalacia).
Differential|Night sweats|NIGHTSWEATS|TUBERCULOSIS LYMPHOMA ENDOCARDITIS HIV MENOPAUSE HYPERTHYROIDISM MEDICATIONS FEVER|Night sweats with weight loss and fever (B symptoms) need evaluation for lymphoma and TB.
Differential|Edema without heart failure|LEGSWELLING|DVT CIRRHOSIS NEPHROTIC AMLODIPINE LYMPHEDEMA VENOUS HYPOTHYROIDISM PREGNANCY|Amlodipine edema comes from arteriolar dilation; diuretics don't help, but adding an ACE inhibitor can.
Differential|Hypercalcemia of malignancy|MALIGNANCYCALCIUM|PTHRP LYTIC CALCITRIOL SQUAMOUS MYELOMA BREAST LYMPHOMA DENOSUMAB|Lymphomas can cause hypercalcemia by making calcitriol, like granulomatous disease.
Differential|Low white count|LEUKOPENIA|VIRAL DRUGS LUPUS FELTY BENIGN DUFFY CLOZAPINE METHIMAZOLE CHEMOTHERAPY|Duffy-null associated neutrophil count is a benign low neutrophil count common in people of African ancestry.
