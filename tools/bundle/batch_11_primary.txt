# Primary care, prevention, geriatrics, women's health
Primary care|Screen it|USPSTF|MAMMOGRAM COLONOSCOPY PAPSMEAR LOWDOSECT DEXASCAN AAA LIPIDS HEPATITISC HIV DEPRESSION|One-time ultrasound screening for abdominal aortic aneurysm in men 65–75 who have ever smoked.
Primary care|Cervical screening|CERVICALCANCER|PAPSMEAR HPV CYTOLOGY COLPOSCOPY TWENTYONE THIRTY HYSTERECTOMY VACCINE|Cervical cancer screening starts at 21; from 30 to 65, HPV testing every 5 years is an option.
Primary care|Breast screening|MAMMOGRAPHY|FORTY BIENNIAL BRCA DENSE TOMOSYNTHESIS MRI FAMILY RISK|USPSTF now recommends biennial screening mammography from age 40 to 74.
Primary care|Bone density|DEXASCAN|TSCORE OSTEOPENIA FRAX SIXTYFIVE FRACTURE HIP SPINE VERTEBRAL|Screen women 65 and older for osteoporosis with DEXA; a T-score of −2.5 or lower is osteoporosis.
Primary care|Shots for grown-ups|ADULTVACCINES|INFLUENZA TDAP ZOSTER PNEUMOCOCCAL HEPATITISB HPV RSV COVID MENINGOCOCCAL|Adults 50 and older get two doses of recombinant zoster vaccine, even if they had shingles before.
Primary care|Shots before travel|TRAVELVACCINES|TYPHOID YELLOWFEVER HEPATITISA RABIES JAPANESE CHOLERA MENINGOCOCCAL POLIO|Yellow fever vaccine is live; avoid it in immunocompromised patients and pregnancy when possible.
Primary care|Live vaccines|LIVEVACCINES|MMR VARICELLA YELLOWFEVER TYPHOID INTRANASAL ROTAVIRUS BCG|Avoid live vaccines in pregnancy and in significantly immunosuppressed patients.
Primary care|Back pain|LOWBACKPAIN|RADICULOPATHY SCIATICA STENOSIS REDFLAGS CAUDA PHYSICALTHERAPY NSAIDS IMAGING|Don't image acute low back pain in the first 6 weeks unless red flags are present.
Primary care|Knee pain|KNEEEXAM|MENISCUS ACL LACHMAN MCMURRAY EFFUSION OSTEOARTHRITIS PATELLOFEMORAL OTTAWA LIGAMENT CRUCIATE|The Lachman test is the most sensitive exam maneuver for an ACL tear.
Primary care|Shoulder pain|ROTATORCUFF|SUPRASPINATUS IMPINGEMENT ADHESIVE CAPSULITIS NEER HAWKINS DROPARM FROZEN|Frozen shoulder (adhesive capsulitis) limits both active and passive motion; it is common in diabetes.
Primary care|Ankle injury|OTTAWARULES|MALLEOLUS NAVICULAR FIFTHMETATARSAL WEIGHTBEARING XRAY SPRAIN TALUS|Ottawa ankle rules: X-ray for malleolar tenderness or inability to bear weight four steps.
Primary care|Wear and tear|OSTEOARTHRITIS|HEBERDEN BOUCHARD CREPITUS JOINTSPACE OSTEOPHYTES WEIGHTLOSS ACETAMINOPHEN KNEE|Heberden nodes (DIP) and Bouchard nodes (PIP) are osteoarthritis; MCP involvement suggests RA or hemochromatosis.
Primary care|Prostate trouble|PROSTATE|HYPERPLASIA TAMSULOSIN FINASTERIDE PSA NOCTURIA RETENTION PROSTATITIS TURP|For BPH, alpha-blockers (tamsulosin) work in days; 5-alpha-reductase inhibitors shrink the prostate over months.
Primary care|Men's health|ERECTILE|DYSFUNCTION SILDENAFIL TADALAFIL NITRATES TESTOSTERONE VASCULAR DIABETES PEYRONIE|Never combine PDE5 inhibitors with nitrates; the combination can cause severe hypotension.
Primary care|Birth control|CONTRACEPTION|ESTROGEN PROGESTIN IUD COPPER IMPLANT INJECTABLE CONDOM EMERGENCY ULIPRISTAL|Avoid estrogen-containing contraception in smokers over 35 and women with migraine with aura.
Primary care|Hot flashes|MENOPAUSE|VASOMOTOR ESTRADIOL PROGESTERONE FEZOLINETANT PAROXETINE VAGINAL ATROPHY OSTEOPOROSIS FLUSHES INSOMNIA|Women with a uterus who take estrogen need a progestin to prevent endometrial cancer.
Primary care|Pregnant patient|PREGNANCY|PREECLAMPSIA GESTATIONAL DIABETES LABETALOL NIFEDIPINE ASPIRIN FOLATE TERATOGENS|ACE inhibitors, ARBs, and warfarin are avoided in pregnancy.
Primary care|Pregnancy and the lungs|MATERNALMEDICINE|ASTHMA DYSPNEA THROMBOEMBOLISM HEPARIN PERIPARTUM AMNIOTIC TOCOLYTIC|Low molecular weight heparin, not warfarin or DOACs, is the anticoagulant of choice in pregnancy.
Geriatrics|Falls|FALLRISK|ORTHOSTATIC VISION BENZODIAZEPINES TIMEDUPANDGO VITAMIND BALANCE NEUROPATHY POLYPHARMACY|Exercise programs focused on balance and strength reduce falls in older adults.
Geriatrics|Frailty|GERIATRICS|POLYPHARMACY DEPRESCRIBING DEMENTIA INCONTINENCE FALLS FRAILTY SARCOPENIA DELIRIUM|Deprescribing reduces drug harms; start with anticholinergics, sedatives, and drugs without a clear indication.
Geriatrics|Leaky bladder|INCONTINENCE|STRESS URGE OVERFLOW FUNCTIONAL KEGEL OXYBUTYNIN MIRABEGRON PESSARY|Urge incontinence: try bladder training first; mirabegron avoids the anticholinergic effects of oxybutynin.
Geriatrics|Memory testing|COGNITION|MOCA MINICOG CLOCKDRAWING RECALL ORIENTATION EXECUTIVE DELIRIUM DEPRESSION|The Mini-Cog combines three-word recall with a clock drawing test.
Geriatrics|Elder abuse|ELDERCARE|NEGLECT EXPLOITATION CAREGIVER BRUISING ISOLATION REPORTING ADULTPROTECTIVE|Unexplained bruises or injuries in an older adult should prompt a private interview and a report to Adult Protective Services.
Primary care|Common cold and friends|COLDSEASON|RHINOVIRUS SINUSITIS OTITIS PHARYNGITIS BRONCHITIS LARYNGITIS ANTIBIOTICS SUPPORTIVE|Acute bronchitis is usually viral; antibiotics don't help, and the cough can last 3 weeks.
Primary care|Sinus infection|SINUSITIS|PURULENT DOUBLESICKENING AMOXICILLIN CLAVULANATE TENDAYS NASALSTEROIDS SALINE|Treat sinusitis with antibiotics only if it lasts over 10 days, is severe, or worsens after improving.
Primary care|Ear pain|OTITIS|MEDIA EXTERNA SWIMMER MALIGNANT PSEUDOMONAS DIABETIC CIPROFLOXACIN TYMPANIC|Malignant otitis externa in older diabetics is Pseudomonas osteomyelitis of the skull base; it needs IV antibiotics.
Primary care|Pink eye|REDEYE|CONJUNCTIVITIS ALLERGIC BACTERIAL VIRAL GLAUCOMA UVEITIS SCLERITIS KERATITIS|Red eye with pain, photophobia, or vision loss needs same-day ophthalmology; simple conjunctivitis has none of these.
Primary care|Lifestyle medicine|LIFESTYLE|EXERCISE MEDITERRANEAN SLEEP SMOKING ALCOHOL WEIGHT STRESS SODIUM|Adults should get at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity a week plus strength training twice a week.
Primary care|Alcohol use|ALCOHOLUSE|AUDIT CAGE NALTREXONE ACAMPROSATE DISULFIRAM GABAPENTIN BINGE WITHDRAWAL THIAMINE|Naltrexone and acamprosate both help alcohol use disorder; acamprosate is safe in liver disease.
Primary care|Fatigue workup|FATIGUE|ANEMIA HYPOTHYROIDISM DEPRESSION SLEEPAPNEA DIABETES CANCER MEDICATIONS VITAMIN|Initial fatigue labs: CBC, metabolic panel, TSH, and glucose; most causes are found by history.
Primary care|Unintended weight loss|WEIGHTLOSS|MALIGNANCY DEPRESSION HYPERTHYROIDISM DIABETES MALABSORPTION DEMENTIA DENTAL|Unintentional weight loss over 5% in 6–12 months needs a workup; cancer, GI disease, and depression lead the list.
Primary care|Swollen glands|LYMPHADENOPATHY|SUPRACLAVICULAR VIRCHOW BIOPSY GENERALIZED LOCALIZED REACTIVE LYMPHOMA HIV|A left supraclavicular (Virchow) node suggests abdominal malignancy; biopsy it.
Primary care|Chem panel|LABVALUES|SODIUM POTASSIUM CHLORIDE BICARBONATE CREATININE GLUCOSE CALCIUM MAGNESIUM|Correct the calcium for albumin: add 0.8 mg/dL for every 1 g/dL that albumin is below 4.
Primary care|Headache red flags|SNOOPCRITERIA|SYSTEMIC NEUROLOGIC ONSET OLDER POSITIONAL PAPILLEDEMA PROGRESSIVE PREGNANCY|Headache red flags (SNOOP): systemic signs, neurologic signs, sudden onset, older age, and pattern change.
Primary care|Bad heartburn signs|ALARMFEATURES|DYSPHAGIA WEIGHTLOSS ANEMIA BLEEDING VOMITING ODYNOPHAGIA MASS|GERD with alarm features (dysphagia, weight loss, bleeding, anemia) needs upper endoscopy.
Primary care|Kids grow up|TRANSITIONCARE|CONGENITAL SICKLECELL CYSTICFIBROSIS TYPEONE PEDIATRIC ADULT CLINIC|Young adults with childhood-onset diseases do better with planned transitions to adult care.
Primary care|Preventive aspirin|ASPIRINUSE|PRIMARY SECONDARY BLEEDING PREVENTION STROKE CORONARY STENT|Aspirin for primary prevention is no longer routine in older adults because bleeding risk offsets benefit.
Primary care|Statin decisions|STATINBENEFIT|ASCVD RISKSCORE LDL DIABETES CALCIUMSCORE LIPOPROTEIN FAMILIAL ROSUVASTATIN|A coronary artery calcium score of zero can support deferring statins in borderline-risk patients.
